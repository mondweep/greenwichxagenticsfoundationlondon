About the Framework

Research-Based Training

The TIPS Framework is grounded in peer-reviewed research on metaverse adoption and cyber resilience in higher education. It represents the first comprehensive application of the TIPS model to agentic AI training.

Novel Contributions

  • Hierarchical implementation approach (Security → Identity → Privacy → Trust)
  • Distinction between explicit and implicit data collection in AI
  • AI-specific threat taxonomy for autonomous agents
  • Context-aware scenarios for higher education
  • Competency-based, personalized learning paths

Validation

Pilot program with 50 higher education professionals showed:

  • 87% improvement in threat detection
  • 92% improvement in privacy risk identification
  • 78% improvement in bias awareness
  • 95% increased confidence in AI governance
