# TIPS Framework for Responsible Agentic AI Training

## Overview

This project implements a comprehensive training framework for teaching individuals to use Generative and Agentic AI responsibly, based on the TIPS framework (Trust, Identity, Privacy, Security).

## Background

Building on research published on:
- **TIPS Framework for Metaverse Adoption** (Computers & Security, 2026)
- **Cyber Resilience in Higher Education**

This framework addresses the critical need for structured training on responsible AI use, particularly for:
- Higher education institutions
- CISOs and security professionals
- Students and faculty
- Organizations adopting agentic AI systems

## The TIPS Framework

### Trust
- Building confidence in AI systems
- Understanding AI capabilities and limitations
- Evaluating AI outputs critically
- Establishing accountability mechanisms

### Identity
- Managing digital identity with AI systems
- Understanding authentication and authorization
- Protecting personal identity information
- Avatar and persona management in AI contexts

### Privacy
- Data collection awareness (explicit vs implicit)
- Personal information protection
- Understanding AI training data implications
- Privacy-preserving AI interactions

### Security
- Secure AI system interactions
- Threat awareness (prompt injection, data leakage)
- Security-by-design principles
- Incident response and resilience

## Key Features

1. **Hierarchical Implementation**: Following research findings, security foundations are established before identity, privacy, and trust considerations
2. **Practical Scenarios**: Real-world examples relevant to higher education and enterprise contexts
3. **Assessment Tools**: Measure understanding and application of TIPS principles
4. **Interactive Learning**: Hands-on demonstrations with agentic AI systems
